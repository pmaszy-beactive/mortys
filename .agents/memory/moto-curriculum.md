# Moto (motorcycle) program business rules

- Program: Theory 1 yard prep (3h) → SAAQ 6R knowledge test (external, either order with Theory 1) → 4 closed-circuit sessions (4h each) → Theory 2 road prep (3h) → 3 road sessions (2h/4h/4h). 2 theory / 7 practical total.
- Closed-circuit sessions require BOTH Theory 1 and an office-recorded 6R pass; Theory 2 requires the full closed circuit; road sessions require Theory 2. Class numbers outside the program and wrong/missing practical durations are rejected (legacy placeholder classes must not be bookable).
- SAAQ closed-track exam, 11-month wait, and final road exam are informational milestones only — not tracked beyond display.

**Why:** the printed SAAQ course-steps document is authoritative; the old placeholder (7 theory + 10 riding, all theory first) must never resurface, and the "all theory before practical" simplified rule now applies to scooter only.
